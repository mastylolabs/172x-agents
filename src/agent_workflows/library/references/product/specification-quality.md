# Specification quality

A complete requirement identifies the actor, observable behavior, condition or trigger, outcome, and acceptance evidence.

- Separate goals, requirements, non-goals, and implementation choices.
- Cover normal, empty, loading, failure, authorization, and recovery states where relevant.
- Prefer examples and testable acceptance criteria over adjectives such as fast or intuitive.
- Keep unresolved decisions visible; do not turn an assumption into an unstated requirement.
