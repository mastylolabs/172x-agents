# Discovery methods

Use these prompts only when the answer materially affects the outcome.

- Who has the problem, and in what situation?
- What outcome should improve, and how will it be observed?
- What is explicitly out of scope?
- What constraints, dependencies, decisions, or risks could invalidate the work?
- What is known from evidence versus assumed?

End with a bounded problem statement, draft success criteria, and the smallest questions requiring human direction.
