# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

172X Agents is committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and professional community. Participation must
be free from harassment and discrimination, including on the basis of age, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, sex characteristics, sexual orientation, or socio-economic status.

We expect contributors to be respectful, constructive, and mindful of the impact of their words
and actions. Harassment, threats, personal attacks, publication of private information, and other
unprofessional conduct are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone represents the 172X Agents community in
public. Mastylo Labs LLC maintainers may clarify expectations, remove or reject content, issue a
warning, suspend participation, or permanently ban a participant when necessary to protect the
community.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to
[zbigniew@mastylolabs.com](mailto:zbigniew@mastylolabs.com). Reports will be handled promptly,
fairly, and with respect for the reporter's privacy.

## Standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), including its Community
Impact Guidelines. This local policy provides the project contact and enforcement context.
